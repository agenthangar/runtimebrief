## RuntimeBrief app icon

The shipped asset is
`RuntimeBrief/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png` with SHA-256
`5f599ca69b42b037b3b23240fd09a55526a5c8078bdea6a7e7ea46f39dce49c3`.

It was generated for this project with OpenAI's image-generation service on
July 11, 2026. The input was text only: no reference image, third-party
artwork, logo, or other third-party asset was supplied.

The selected generation used this prompt:

> Create a premium minimalist app icon showing three peripheral agent nodes
> connected into one central node, with the negative space subtly forming a
> capital B without making the letter obvious. Convey consolidation and
> intelligence. Use crisp, vector-friendly geometric shapes, a deep midnight
> navy background, a warm off-white central mark, and electric cyan and cobalt
> node accents. Do not include explicit typography, words, tiny details, a
> device mockup, transparency, an outer rounded-square border, a molecule or
> blockchain cliché, a chat bubble, a robot, or a watermark.

1024 pixels for the iOS asset catalog. The maintainer authorizes its
distribution under the repository's MIT terms. OpenAI's current
[Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/) state that, as
between OpenAI and the user and to the extent permitted by applicable law, the
user owns the output; they also note that generated output may not be unique.

The former `AppIconSource.svg` described an earlier, superseded speech-bubble
icon and was removed so it would not be mistaken for the source of the shipped
artwork.
